---

# PURPOSE

PARSER_v1 defines the complete operational parsing behavior for SRP.

SRP does not parse text only.

SRP parses:

semantic-operational pedagogical continuity.

The parser is designed for:

* pedagogical operational memory
* multicourse restructuring
* longitudinal continuity preservation
* resilient semantic extraction
* operational future usability
* deterministic operational consistency

The parser prioritizes:

* semantic preservation
* pedagogical continuity
* operational continuity
* structural usability
* deterministic consistency

over:

* strict textual literalism
* original narrative order
* superficial cleanup
* stylistic rewriting
* creative reinterpretation

---

# INPUT ASSUMPTIONS

The parser MUST assume inputs may contain:

* oral transcription artifacts
* fragmented narration
* abrupt topic switching
* multicourse interleaving
* partial references
* ambiguous phrasing
* incomplete sentences
* emotionally contextual language
* transcription corruption
* repeated ideas
* exploratory language
* implicit continuity
* semantic fragmentation

The parser MUST remain resilient under:

* chaotic narrative order
* partial operational context
* semantic fragmentation
* imperfect transcription
* contextual ambiguity

---

# PIPELINE PRIORITY

The parser MUST prioritize the following operational pipeline:

1. course detection
2. semantic segmentation
3. operational classification
4. continuity derivation
5. output assembly

Course detection MUST occur BEFORE semantic redistribution.

Semantic redistribution MUST occur BEFORE final category assembly.

The parser MAY generate:

* warnings
* ambiguity flags
* continuity uncertainty markers

Warnings are:

* parser metadata
* ambiguity indicators
* parser diagnostics

Warnings are NOT:

* operational persistence
* pending actions
* operational categories
* derived continuity

---

# CORE ARCHITECTURAL PRINCIPLES

## 1. COURSE REORGANIZATION

The parser reorganizes content completely by course.

Narrative order does NOT dominate final operational structure.

Operational course grouping has priority over original narration sequence.

---

## 2. CONSERVATIVE INTERNAL CONTINUITY

Within each course:

* semantic continuity MUST be preserved conservatively
* related ideas SHOULD remain contextually connected
* redistribution MUST preserve pedagogical intent

The parser MUST avoid:

* destructive semantic fragmentation
* unrelated regrouping
* contextual continuity loss

---

## 3. SEMANTIC REDISTRIBUTION

The parser MAY redistribute content within a course ONLY IF:

* original intent is preserved
* operational usability improves
* semantic continuity remains intact
* no new content is invented
* pedagogical meaning remains stable

The parser MUST NOT:

* invent pedagogical logic
* fabricate continuity
* aggressively reorganize ambiguous material

---

## 4. ANTI-OVERINFERENCE

The parser MUST NOT:

* prescribe pedagogy
* invent strategies
* invent tasks
* create methodologies
* psychologize participants
* emotionally reinterpret narration
* transform contextual improvisations into persistent methodology
* create artificial continuity

The parser MAY infer continuity ONLY IF:

* future continuity is explicitly implied
* unresolved pedagogical progression exists
* operational follow-up is contextually necessary
* continuity derivation does not require invented pedagogy

---

# SEMANTIC UNIT POLICY

The minimum parseable unit is hybrid-semantic.

The parser MUST avoid:

* microtask fragmentation
* excessive atomization
* giant narrative blocks
* paragraph-level persistence without redistribution

The parser SHOULD:

* preserve functional context
* separate operationally distinct actions
* maintain semantic proximity between related actions
* preserve pedagogical continuity

Example:

Input:

"Hacer PPT con merengues para cantar"

Possible operational segmentation:

* preparar PPT merengues
* iniciar canto merengues

The parser MUST preserve the functional relationship between both actions.

---

# OPERATIONAL CLASSIFICATION

The parser MUST assign:

* one primary operational category

The parser MAY assign:

* secondary semantic relations

Primary category selection MUST prioritize:

* operational intent

over:

* thematic keywords
* superficial semantic similarity
* artistic similarity

The parser MUST avoid:

* artificial duplication
* category multiplication
* redundant persistence

---

# PERSISTENCE THRESHOLD

The parser MUST distinguish between:

* contextual observation
* operational continuity
* strategic persistence
* exploratory thought
* contextual improvisation
* partial progress state
* persistent methodology

The parser MUST NOT transform:

* emotional reactions
* isolated observations
* contextual improvisations
* temporary fixes
* narrative commentary

into automatic operational persistence.

Persistence MUST occur ONLY IF:

* future intention exists explicitly
* unresolved operational continuity exists
* pedagogical progression remains unresolved
* contextual reuse potential exists explicitly

---

# DIFFERENTIAL PERSISTENCE

Persistence behavior varies depending on operational category family.

Persistence MUST NOT obey a single global policy.

## 1. IMMEDIATE-OPERATIONAL CATEGORIES

Examples:

* 🎵 pendientes_sala
* 🎒 pendientes_materiales

These categories:

* require explicit or strongly implied continuity
* require near-term operational relevance
* require contextual operational intent

These categories MUST NOT preserve:

* vague ideas
* abstract exploration
* open speculation
* operationally disconnected possibilities

---

## 2. PEDAGOGICAL-DESIGN CATEGORIES

Examples:

* 🧠 pendientes_planificacion
* 🎼 posible_repertorio

These categories:

* allow future-oriented persistence
* allow open pedagogical design continuity
* tolerate limited exploration

BUT require:

* explicit pedagogical intent
* plausible pedagogical continuity
* sufficient contextual operationality

---

## 3. EXPLORATORY-DEVELOPMENT CATEGORIES

Examples:

* 💻 pendientes_apps

These categories:

* MAY preserve long-range exploratory ideas
* MAY preserve speculative operational exploration
* MAY preserve non-immediate future architecture

These categories tolerate:

* distant continuity
* open exploration
* partial future operational intent

---

## 4. LONGITUDINAL-CONTEXT CATEGORIES

Examples:

* 📝 revision

These categories:

* preserve pedagogical contextual memory
* do NOT require future operational continuity
* do NOT require derived actions

These categories MAY preserve:

* successful strategies
* longitudinal observations
* partial progress states
* observed methodological changes
* pedagogically useful context

---

# INFERENCE POLICY

The parser MAY infer:

* implicit pedagogical continuity
* operational follow-up continuity
* unresolved progression continuity
* clearly derived operational needs
* longitudinal contextual continuity

The parser MUST NOT infer:

* new teaching strategies
* unexpressed decisions
* speculative intentions
* concrete pedagogical plans
* unexplained methodology
* specific didactic solutions

Exploratory language such as:

* quizás
* podría
* tal vez
* a lo mejor

MUST NOT generate automatic persistence.

Exception:

* 💻 pendientes_apps MAY preserve exploratory long-range development ideas.

---

# STRATEGY PRESERVATION

The parser MAY preserve:

* successful tactics
* functional classroom dynamics
* reusable pedagogical configurations
* participation-improving strategies
* operationally successful structures

ONLY IF:

* future operational value exists
* contextual continuity exists
* operational reuse plausibility exists
* the observation is not trivial

---

# IMPROVISATION VS PERSISTENCE

The parser MUST distinguish:

* contextual improvisation
  vs
* persistent strategy

Observed adaptation does NOT automatically become future methodology.

Temporary operational resolution does NOT automatically become operational persistence.

---

# ABSENCE AND PARTICIPATION

The parser MAY preserve:

* relevant absences
* participation continuity
* turn rotation
* continuity interruption due to absence
* participation distribution

These observations do NOT automatically constitute:

* conduct problems
* institutional intervention
* administrative escalation

---

# MULTICOURSE AMBIGUITY

If course attribution cannot be resolved safely:

* ambiguity MUST be preserved
* unsafe redistribution MUST be avoided
* cross-course contamination MUST be prevented
* unresolved course attribution MAY be preserved explicitly

The parser MUST NOT:

* invent course ownership
* merge ambiguous courses
* aggressively redistribute ambiguous content

---

# IMPERFECT TRANSCRIPTION HANDLING

The parser MUST assume:

* transcription corruption
* misspelled names
* partially recognized songs
* incomplete references
* phonetic corruption
* lexical ambiguity

The parser MUST prioritize:

* resilience
* conservative recovery
* contextual preservation
* semantic continuity

The parser MUST NOT:

* hallucinate aggressive corrections
* creatively reinterpret ambiguous phrases
* destructively normalize corrupted content

---

# REPRESENTATION LAYERING

Parser output represents:

* machine-safe operational structure

Parser output does NOT necessarily represent:

* final human-readable rendering
* final pedagogical visualization
* user-facing operational formatting

Human-readable rendering MAY:

* expand normalized structures
* restore contextual readability
* reconstruct narrative continuity
* improve operational readability

Machine representation and human representation MUST be treated as separate layers.

---

# DETERMINISTIC CONSISTENCY

Given equivalent semantic input,
the parser SHOULD produce semantically stable output.

The parser MUST prioritize:

* structural consistency
* semantic continuity
* operational predictability
* parser stability

over:

* creative reinterpretation
* excessive restructuring
* aggressive inference

---

# FAILURE HANDLING

When safe semantic resolution cannot be achieved:

* ambiguity preservation has priority over unsafe inference
* continuity uncertainty MUST remain explicit
* parser stability has priority over aggressive completion

The parser SHOULD prefer:

* incomplete but safe structure

over:

* hallucinated operational certainty

---

# OUTPUT EXPECTATIONS

The parser output MUST:

* preserve operational usability
* preserve semantic continuity
* preserve pedagogical continuity
* remain structurally deterministic
* remain machine-safe
* remain resilient to noisy input

The parser MUST avoid:

* semantic collapse
* category contamination
* overfragmentation
* artificial continuity
* hallucinated pedagogy

---

# FINAL PRIORITY

The parser MUST prioritize:

semantic-operational preservation

over:

* literal textual preservation
* original narrative order
* stylistic fidelity
* superficial cleanup
* creative rewriting
